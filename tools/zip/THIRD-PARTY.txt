OBSPETS's built-in pet sounds (data/sounds/) are not covered by OBSPETS's GPL. The
default set's voice was synthesised for OBSPETS. The other sounds were edited from
royalty-free sound libraries (the Sonniss #GameAudioGDC bundles and the Fab
Standard License) and ship as part of OBSPETS under those licenses: use them with
OBSPETS, but don't extract them to sell or share as a sound library.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Bundled characters made from others' art (data/packs/, each with a LICENSE.txt):

- cat-ginger, cat-black, cat-siamese, cat-sphynx, cat-fluffy, cat-tabby: Pet Cats
  Pack by LuizMelo (https://luizmelo.itch.io/pet-cat-pack), CC0.
- dog-retriever, dog-akita, dog-great-dane, dog-schnauzer, dog-saint-bernard, dog-husky:
  Pet Dogs Pack by LuizMelo (https://luizmelo.itch.io/pet-dogs-pack), CC0.
